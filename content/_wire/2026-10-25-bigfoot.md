*[Capturing Bigfoot](https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0JEZQXCK3S1WPFI4PVLLPBFKIB)* is a great documentary. Completely worth the $15 ticket to rent it, especially if you watch it with family. Go in to learn the truth about the infamous Bigfoot film. Come out having watched one of the most touching stories about human love and family I’ve ever seen.
