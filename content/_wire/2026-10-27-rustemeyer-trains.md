> The family started small, replacing a small oval of grass in the center of the backyard with tracks. The railroad grew every year, eventually filling the entire yard.
> 
> Now it can accommodate seven trains at once. Sometimes the Rustemeyers even put a plow on the front of the trains so they can run in the snow.
> 
> On a recent Saturday, neighbors and fellow train enthusiasts watched as the locomotives chugged past miniature shops, small people, a water tower, a cemetery and brightly colored rose bushes. …
> 
> The Rustemeyers also invite neighbors over any time they run the trains.
> 
> “At night the lights go on and it’s very relaxing. People love to just sit and watch it,” Shirley Rustemeyer said.
