Just fucking awful.

> An estimated 90,000 Bedouins live in dozens of unrecognized villages across the Negev … Many of them pre-date the creation of Israel in 1948 … A bureaucratic maze of Israeli agencies and decades of disputes over land ownership have inhibited a solution that would allow for their legalization …
> The demolitions affected branches of Ziadna’s family that suffered devastating losses following the Oct. 7 attack. His cousin, also named Youssef al-Ziadna, was abducted to Gaza with three of his children. Two were released after 55 days, but the father and another son, Hamza, were killed in captivity. The late hostage’s brother and sister have since had homes and other structures demolished …
> The eviction notice … gives the occupants 30 days to vacate … [but] even those deadlines were not always respected. In one case … officers from the Yoav Unit … arrived just a week after a notice was posted and demolished a relative’s home …
> Ziadna, who is a father of seven, dismantled a warehouse on his property after receiving a demolition order rather than face the fines of having the authorities demolish it …

> “After everything we did on Oct. 7, running around and risking our lives, doing whatever we could, none of it has any meaning,” he told the Jewish Telegraphic Agency. “In the end, you’re still an Arab.”
