If you’ve tried to use an Apogee Symphony Thunderbridge with macOS Tahoe and haven’t been able to get it to work, try restarting and then going to System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll to the bottom, look for something like “extensions need your permission,” and grant permission for the Apogee driver to run. Your Symphony isn’t bricked, yet.
