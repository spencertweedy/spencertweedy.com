---
title: Kitchen Shakedown
---

Here’s a silly little thing I want to complain about, on the eve of (rumored) economic upheaval in the US.

You buy an Alexa Show. You pay Alexa for the screen, the motherboard, all of it. (Whether they sold it at a loss is their problem.)

It collects data about you and your habits, which Amazon uses to make more money.

Then, if you want to display pictures of your family on the device’s screen, instead of advertisements, they want you to pay? $2.99 every month?

To put photos on the screen that you bought and installed in your home.

Obscene.

(It’s a silly little thing, but it’s related to a handful of serious big things.)
