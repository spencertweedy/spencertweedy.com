---
title: Manual Promotion
---

It’s that time, after making a project, where I get anxious about how to publicize it.

I think Manual Play has some potential. I get so much use out of it already, and people seem to have a positive response to it. And I want to do right by its potential!

My inclination when faced with uncertainty like that is to keep it humble, keep it simple, and just… tell you (blog readers) about the app. But that wouldn’t be as fun as a wide release.

So the answer is to take a breath and take the steps that would actually allow it to find people who don’t already know me, but those steps aren’t completely clear, and some of them are uncomfortable.
