title: A Chair Designed by Someone Who’s Never Sat in One

Right after I wrote about wishing chairs into existence, I read James Bridle’s essay “[Benchma(r)king](https://emergencemagazine.org/essay/benchmarking/).”

> Give a clever machine some widget, like a structural bracket or a complex workflow, and it will produce some alien-looking thing, a third of the weight and twice as efficient, which a human mind would not have conceived. That’s cool, that’s interesting. Of course, applied to culture, society, and the wider environment, such an idea of efficiency is a disaster, but it’s pretty neat for objects, as long as a human is keeping an eye on things …
> What we have here is, quite literally, a chair designed by someone who has read a lot about chairs but never actually sat in one.
