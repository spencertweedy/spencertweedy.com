title: "Producing a Record Is Like"
show_title: true
---

Producing a record is like:

Oh my god, this is impossible. It’s not gonna sound good. I have no idea how to make that idea happen. It’s too hard to plug all these cables in. The piano is out of tune. I blasted out my ears. My suggestions are stale. I suck at guitar. I suck at drums. The vocals are thin. I haven’t drank water since 10am. I’m starving. My family thinks I’m missing. I forgot my name. I forgot my age. Am I dreaming or awake? Am I going to die… now?

Then:

Oh my god I love this song.
