Scary but well-written two sentences in the *NYTimes*:

> Somehow, we’ve made the industrial equivalent of a Fabergé egg the linchpin of our civilization. What would happen if someone started breaking them?

The eggs in question are the oil-filled transformers that are used in all the power distribution stations around the world, which have a wait time of five years or more to replace, and which, on a few occassions, unidentified, uncaught gunmen have destroyed by remotely poking holes in them.

I’m reading *Parable of the Sower* right now and already felt like it was too close for comfort. Now even more so.

But, if Silicon Valley succeeds in making solid-state transformers to help power data centers, then we won’t have to worry (as much) about the *NYTimes*’ hypothetical nine-transformer years-long blackout.
