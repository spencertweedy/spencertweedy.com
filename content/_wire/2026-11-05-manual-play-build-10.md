title: Manual Play Build 10
---

Changes in [Manual Play](https://testflight.apple.com/join/f4y22UjJ) test build 10:

* Faster first sync
* Tappable previews for the sites in Browse Sources
* Also categorized em as Individual Writers, Music Blogs, Magazines, and Newspapers
* You choose when a release gets called “Historic”: 1, 2, 5, 10, or 20 years
* Up and down buttons to navigate between releases pages
* Sync status shows up beneath each screen’s title
* Open in Spotify is more reliable
* Credits for “Traditional,” “Various Artists,” and other catch-alls won’t trigger huge credits-info downloads anymore

Thanks to Kevin M. and everyone who’s submitted feedback.
