title: Fear of Meaning
show_title: true
---

A phrase popped into my head the other day: fear of meaning.

When someone’s talking but it seems like they’re bending over backwards to avoid saying *anything* at all. The vaguest terms, the platitudinousiest platitudes, the broadest brush strokes.

They don’t do it on purpose. They do it because it’s scary to take a stand and really just say something? I don’t really understand. But it repulses me, so I really don’t want to do it myself.
