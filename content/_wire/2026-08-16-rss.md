I’m using RSS again, reading websites I like with [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com) (free and open source).

Turns out most things I want to read on the internet still publish RSS and the apps for reading it, like NetNewsWire, are better than ever.

No pop-ups… No annoying shit… No algorithm… No dick-enlargement ads… Dip in as much or as little as you want.

Some good ideas stick around.
