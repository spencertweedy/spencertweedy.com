One thing I’ve realized about working on long-term projects is that you have to figure out how to feel accomplished every once in a while. Otherwise it’s hard to stay motivated. Sometimes the feeling of accomplishment can come from inside the project, other times you gotta get it elsewhere. Something as little as taking a shower and shaving can make you feel like you’re not a failure. You know, the old “clean yourself, love yourself” routine.
